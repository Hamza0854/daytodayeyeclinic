Cataract

The cloudy natural lens is removed and replaced with a clear artificial one, restoring sharper, brighter vision.

Pterygium

Removal of the fleshy growth that spreads over the white of the eye, often linked to sun and dust.

Glaucoma

Procedures that lower the pressure inside the eye when drops alone aren't enough.

See what's happening at the back of the eye.

Glaucoma and retinal disease often cause no pain and no early symptoms. Our scanners find the changes years before your sight does.

OCT

Optical coherence tomography

A quick, non-contact light scan that shows the retina and optic nerve in fine layers, used for glaucoma, macular disease and diabetic eye checks.

VFT

Visual field test

You press a button when you see small lights appear around the edge of your vision, mapping any blind spots you may not know you have.

Both tests are painless and non-invasive.
